Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026.

Who

The catai publishing tool is private software used by its owner to post videos to the catai TikTok account. It has no public users and collects nothing from the public.

What the tool stores

When the owner authorizes the tool with TikTok, TikTok issues an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID, display name and avatar. These are stored on the owner's own computer, in a private file, and are used only to publish videos to that account and to show which account is being posted to.

What the tool sends to TikTok

The video file, its caption, the privacy setting, the interaction settings, the AI-generated content flag and the commercial content disclosure the owner selects for each post.

What the tool does not do

It does not collect data from viewers, does not track anyone, uses no cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes the tool's access in TikTok's settings or deletes the token file. Either action ends all access immediately.
